Clinician-to-clinician genetics eConsults

Genetics expertise for every clinician in the Southeast.

SEGMENT (SouthEastern Genetics & Genomics Medicine Econsult NeTwork) links healthcare providers in North Carolina, South Carolina, and Georgia with geneticists and genetic counselors through secure electronic consultations. Your patients get expert guidance without waiting for a genetics appointment.

Why SEGMENT

Genetic testing is everywhere. Genetics expertise isn't.

Clinicians in every specialty now order and receive genetic tests, but there are few genetics specialists, and most work at academic centers. Many clinical questions don't need a clinic visit. They need a quick answer from an expert.

Long waits for genetics clinics

Patients often wait months for a first appointment, which delays diagnosis and management.

Hours of travel

Rural families may have to drive hours to reach the nearest genetics service, which is a real barrier to care.

Results that are hard to act on

Variants of uncertain significance and complex reports are hard to translate into next steps without specialist support.

What we do

A regional network that brings genetics to the point of care
