About genetics eConsults

What is a genetics eConsult?

A genetics eConsult is a secure, asynchronous consultation between clinicians. You send a question about a specific patient, and a geneticist or genetic counselor at a SEGMENT partner site sends back written, patient-specific recommendations.

There's no appointment to schedule and your patient doesn't need to be present. You keep caring for your patient and get specialist support when you need it. If the case does need an in-person genetics evaluation, the eConsult team will tell you and help arrange the referral.

A genetics eConsult can help you:

  • Determine whether a genetics referral is appropriate
  • Select the most appropriate first-line genetic test
  • Interpret a genetic test result
  • Identify additional evaluation or management recommendations
  • Receive timely expert guidance

The benefits

Why request a genetics eConsult?

eConsults get specialist knowledge to the point of care quickly, so you can act with confidence and your patients avoid unnecessary waits.

Faster answers

Get genetics expertise quickly so you can make timely clinical decisions.

Improve patient access

Many questions can be answered without your patient traveling or waiting for a genetics appointment.

Better testing decisions

Order the right test the first time and avoid unnecessary or duplicate testing.

Keep care local

Manage patients in your own practice whenever appropriate, with support from genetics specialists.

Secure communication

All communication happens in secure systems and is documented for continuity of care.

Step by step

How eConsults work

Each partner site runs its own submission workflow, and the overall process is the same in every state.

  1. Set up access

    Find the partner site for your state and create an account if one is required.

  2. Submit your question

    Send one or two focused questions about your patient.

  3. Attach supporting info

    Add notes, lab results, prior genetic tests, family history, or other documents.

  4. Expert review

    A geneticist or genetic counselor reviews the case and sends back recommendations.

  5. Follow up

    Keep the secure conversation going, or refer for a genetics evaluation if needed.

What to ask

Common eConsult topics

Any primary care or specialty clinician can submit an eConsult. These are the kinds of questions we see most often.

Rare disease diagnosis

Suspect a genetic condition? Get help deciding whether to test and which test to order first: a panel, exome, genome, or no test at all.

  • Does this child's presentation warrant genetic testing?
  • Should I order a gene panel or exome sequencing?

Interpreting genetic test results

Variants of uncertain significance, carrier results, secondary findings, and direct-to-consumer reports: what they mean for your patient.

  • How should I counsel my patient about this VUS?
  • Is follow-up family testing recommended?

Managing a diagnosed rare disease

Surveillance schedules, specialist referrals, and management recommendations for patients who already have a genetic diagnosis.

  • What surveillance does this patient with Lynch syndrome need?
  • Which specialists should follow this patient?

Following up genomic screening

Next steps after an abnormal result from population genomic screening or another screening program.

  • My patient screened positive for a BRCA2 variant. What now?
  • Does this result need confirmatory testing?

eConsult guidance

Best practices for a successful eConsult

The more information you include up front, the faster and more specific our recommendations can be.

  • Ask one or two focused clinical questions.
  • Include your assessment and the relevant history.
  • Upload any prior genetic test results, lab reports, imaging, or clinical notes.
  • Include pertinent family history.
  • Explain the decision you are trying to make.
  • Get your patient's consent before submitting.

Choosing the right path

eConsults vs. referrals

When should I submit a genetics eConsult?

An eConsult is ideal when you need guidance like:

  • Which genetic test should I order?
  • Does this patient meet criteria for a genetics evaluation?
  • How should I interpret this genetic test result?
  • What additional workup is recommended?
  • Is additional family testing recommended?

When is a genetics referral more appropriate?

An in-person consultation is generally better when:

  • The patient needs comprehensive genetic counseling.
  • A full diagnostic evaluation is needed.
  • Ongoing genetics management is expected.
  • A physical examination is necessary.
  • Several complex issues need an extensive assessment.

If an eConsult shows that your patient needs an appointment, the partner site will help arrange the referral.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can submit an eConsult?

Healthcare providers in North Carolina, South Carolina, and Georgia, in any primary care or specialty area, who are caring for a patient who may benefit from genetics expertise.

Can clinicians outside North Carolina, South Carolina, and Georgia request an eConsult?

Not through this program, although additional programs are available depending on the region.

Does my patient need to have been seen by a genetics clinic first?

No. eConsults are for patients who have never had a genetics evaluation as well as for those who need follow-up guidance.

Is there a cost for a genetics eConsult?

Greenwood Genetic Center eConsults are free for referring providers. SEGMENT funding supports genetics eConsults throughout the project for UNC Health and Emory Healthcare.

Can I ask follow-up questions?

Yes. You can keep the secure conversation going until the consultation is complete.

What information should I include?

Include as much as you can: the clinical question, medical history, family history, previous genetic test results, relevant lab or imaging results, and clinical notes. The more we have up front, the faster and more specific our recommendations can be.

Can an eConsult replace a genetics appointment?

Sometimes. Many questions can be answered through an eConsult. If your patient needs a comprehensive genetic evaluation, we'll recommend one and help arrange the referral.

Can I order genetic testing after an eConsult?

Yes. Recommendations often include an appropriate first-tier test and guidance on next steps. [Add site-specific details on lab ordering and sample collection kits.]

Is the communication secure and documented?

Yes. eConsults take place in secure, HIPAA-compliant systems, and each consultation is documented for continuity of care.

Can patients request an eConsult directly?

No. eConsults are clinician-to-clinician. If you're a patient, ask your healthcare provider whether an eConsult could help.

How is eConsult information used in SEGMENT research?

SEGMENT will use de-identified summary data to learn about eConsults currently being performed, take steps to expand access for eConsults, and evaluate improvements in eConsult uptake and impact on patient care outcomes.
