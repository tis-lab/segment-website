Before you submit

What to include

The more information you include up front, the faster and more specific the recommendations can be. Learn more about eConsults.

  • Ask one or two focused clinical questions.
  • Include your assessment and the relevant history.
  • Upload any prior genetic test results, lab reports, imaging, or clinical notes.
  • Include pertinent family history.
  • Explain the decision you are trying to make.
  • Get your patient's consent before submitting.
